Our Story

Fresh daily.
Belmont to Geelong.

At Francis Deli, we believe in the simple perfection of a well-made sandwich. Newly opened on Francis Street in Belmont, we bring classic deli cuts, modern bites and good coffee to locals across Belmont and Geelong.

Every morning, our artisanal bread is baked fresh. We partner with local purveyors to source the highest quality meats, cheeses, and produce, ensuring that every bite tells a story of care and craftsmanship.

Local FAQ

Good to know

Quick answers for Belmont and Geelong locals planning a coffee, sandwich or takeaway lunch visit.

Where is Francis Deli?

Francis Deli is at 164 Francis St, Belmont VIC 3216, serving locals across Belmont and Geelong.

What are the opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday, 8:00am–2:30pm, and Saturday to Sunday, 8:30am–2:30pm.

Do you offer takeaway?

Yes. We offer takeaway sandwiches, toasties, coffee, drinks and easy lunch options from our Belmont shop.

Do you offer catering?

For sandwich and lunch catering enquiries around Belmont and Geelong, call us on 0404 962 812.

What food does Francis Deli serve?

Fresh sandwiches, toasties, rolls, coffee, drinks and takeaway lunch — made for Belmont locals and visitors from across Geelong.
